Privacy notice

What's the Craic · last updated 7 November 2026

What's the Craic is a free guide to events in Northern Ireland, run by Peter Summers. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and what you can do about it. If you have a question, email petesummers7130@gmail.com.

Using the app

You don't need an account and we don't ask who you are. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

A few features remember things on your own device, in your browser's storage, so they still work when you come back:

  • events you save with the ★ button
  • venues and searches you follow
  • which events you've already seen, so new ones can be marked "New"

This information stays in your browser. It isn't sent to us and we can't see it. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website.

"Near me"

If you tap "Near me", your browser asks whether to share your location. If you agree, your location is used on your device only, to pick the nearest area of Northern Ireland. It is not sent to us or stored.

Adding an event

If you send us an event through the "Add your event" form, we receive what you type: the event name, date, time, venue, town, link, description and, if you choose to give it, your email address.

  • We use the event details to check the event and, if we approve it, to show it in the app. Everything except your email address may then be shown publicly.
  • We use your email address only to contact you about that event. It is never shown in the app or shared with anyone else.
  • We keep submissions for 3 months after the event and then delete them.

We rely on our legitimate interest in running an accurate events guide to process these details, and on your choice to send them.

Other services the app uses

To work, the app connects to a few other services. When it does, your browser's internet address is visible to them, as it is with any website:

  • Netlify, which hosts this website and receives event submissions
  • Google Fonts, for the app's lettering
  • Open-Meteo, for weather forecasts
  • Ticketmaster and other ticket sellers, only if you follow a link to buy tickets. Their own privacy policies apply on their sites.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how we use it. To do any of these, email petesummers7130@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change how the app uses information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
